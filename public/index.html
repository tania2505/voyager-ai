How the agent works

  1. Validate: checks the request and lists any missing information before spending tool calls.
  2. Search: calls flight, hotel and transport tools, passing travelers, dates and area as arguments.
  3. Filter & compare: applies hard constraints (nonstop, star rating), then scores every combination against the budget.
  4. Decide: picks the best fit, or reports the cheapest option and how far over budget it is.
  5. Itinerary: builds a day-by-day plan from your interests.
  6. Hold & confirm: creates a booking hold and waits. Booking only executes after you confirm.
